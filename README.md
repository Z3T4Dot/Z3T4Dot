## 🧠 Engineering Approach

I care about more than making software work.

I try to understand **why the system exists, how its components interact, what happens when something fails, and how it can evolve without becoming unmaintainable.**

My engineering principles:

- **Architecture before implementation**
- **Security by design**
- **Explicit boundaries between services**
- **Automation over repetitive manual work**
- **Observability over assumptions**
- **Simple solutions before unnecessary complexity**
- **Documentation as part of the system**
- **Failure scenarios are part of the design**

I prefer learning by building real systems and deliberately testing their failure modes.

"It works."

"Why does it work?"

"What happens when it fails?"

"How do I detect the failure?"

"How do I investigate it?"

"How do I recover?"

"How can I automate the solution?"

This is why many of my repositories are structured as labs, experiments and documented engineering projects, rather than simple code demonstrations.
